## Report-first response (current workflow)

1. Administrators/officials register real teams ahead of time through Response → Create Team. Optional west/south/east/north bounds define a rectangular coverage area; either supply all four or leave all blank. Blank bounds mean unknown coverage, not guaranteed regional coverage. No seed data is required.
2. A citizen submits a report with location/coordinates, description and optional media. GPS accuracy is retained when browser geolocation supplies it; manually changing coordinates clears that accuracy. Submission does not contact emergency services automatically. No emergency phone number is fabricated.
3. The official selects **Plan response** on an open report, before or after verification. One screen displays the report, media, coordinates, location description, reported accuracy (or Not provided), review/clarification history and teams. No monitoring location or model assessment is required.
4. Inspect the pin and description. The bundled geographic reference is not street navigation. The official may correct coordinates through numeric inputs or map click, but must supply a correction reason. Original report coordinates remain unchanged and are retained in the deployment snapshot.
5. Review possible duplicates: the heuristic flags up to ten other same-category reports within 500 metres and ±24 hours of submission. These are candidates only; nothing is automatically merged, suppressed or assigned. The same report cannot have two concurrent active assignments through report-first dispatch.
6. Enter a meaningful review note. **Verify only**, **Reject** and **Request clarification** remain available. A citizen can respond to a clarification request on their own report; it returns to Awaiting Verification with the response preserved. No team is needed for verification.
7. Select an available team and explicitly confirm **both destination and team**. For unknown team coverage, the official must judge suitability; configured coverage bounds are enforced on the backend. The screen distinguishes no teams configured, all teams busy and no available team covering the destination. Refresh response reloads current status without clearing notes.
8. **Verify and assign** atomically verifies the report and reserves the team, writing separate audit entries. If the team was taken or any transaction step fails, neither verification nor reservation is committed. Notes are retained in the interface when assignment fails.
9. Alternatively, select **Urgent unverified dispatch**, enter an explicit reason (at least ten characters), and confirm the destination/team. It reserves a team for investigation while retaining the report's unverified status. Urgent dispatch does not itself verify the event or publish a warning.
10. Every new assignment starts **Assigned** (reserved, not departed). An official or administrator explicitly records **En Route → On Site → Completed**, or cancels. Completion/cancellation releases the team. Historical En Route/On Site deployments remain supported without rewriting their history. Citizens see the response status of their own report; deployment lists and movement controls remain restricted to officials/admins.
11. The deployment retains the report ID, original and dispatch coordinates, any correction reason, review history, verification status at assignment, urgent reason if applicable and actor/time history. Administrator audit records distinguish verification, urgent investigation, assignment, correction and movement. Completing a deployment does not automatically resolve a hazard or publish a warning.

This is in-app coordination, not an integration with a live emergency dispatch centre. Teams and coverage must be entered truthfully; use explicitly simulated teams only for presentation practice.

## Inline team registration

If no team is configured, an authorised official/administrator can select **Register a team here** on the incident screen. Enter the actual team name and information source; explicitly mark a practice team when applicable. Saving registers an available team and selects it without leaving the report or clearing the corrected destination/review note. It does not confirm the team, assign it or contact anyone. Coverage remains unknown until configured; destination/team confirmation is still required before assignment. Corrected coordinates remain deliberate user changes with a required reason. Validation: three focused browser cases passed (normal dispatch, urgent dispatch and inline creation preserving inputs), along with build and 419-key translation checks. See `evidence/inline-team-setup.json`.
